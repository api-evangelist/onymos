Onymos, developer of the world’s first Features-as-a-Service platform, and Vapotherm, Inc. (OTCQX: VAPO), (“Vapotherm”), a global medical technology company whose proprietary high-velocity therapy® products treat patients of all ages suffering from respiratory distress, today announced the formation of a development and commercialization partnership. This partnership will help Vapotherm create a unique respiratory digital platform to meet its data visualization and device management goals for the Vapotherm HVT® 2.0 system in the acute care space as well as for future products in the home.
