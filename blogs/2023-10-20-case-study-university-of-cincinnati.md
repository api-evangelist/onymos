Pre-COVID, the non-profit association EDUCAUSE reported that only 19% of higher education institutions leveraged “digital transformation” technologies like OCR (optical character recognition). Post-COVID, things have changed. And some of the University of Cincinnati’s newest leaders are the ones leading the charge.
